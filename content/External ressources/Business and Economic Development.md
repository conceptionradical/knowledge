## ⚖️ Corporate & Legal Guides

* **[Fasken - Establishing a Business in Québec](https://www.fasken.com/en/knowledge/doing-business-in-canada/16-establishing-a-business-in-quebec)** — Comprehensive legal overview and guide covering corporate structuring, registration, regulatory compliance, and business formation requirements in the province of Québec. 

* **[Norton Rose Fulbright - Doing Business in Québec: Tax Considerations](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-ca/knowledge/publications/10323538/doing-business-in-quebec-tax-considerations)** — Comprehensive legal overview of corporate taxation, tax incentives, employer obligations, and compliance frameworks for operating or expanding a business within Québec.

* **[Norton Rose Fulbright - Doing Business in Québec: Privacy Legislation](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-ca/knowledge/publications/98bd3466/doing-business-in-quebec-privacy-legislation)** — Legal analysis of Québec's strict data protection framework (Law 25), covering mandatory corporate compliance, data governance policies, transparency requirements, and cross-border transfer obligations.
