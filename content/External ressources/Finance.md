## 💰 Canadian Financial Calculators & Planning Tools


* **[TD Net Worth Calculator](https://tools.td.com/Net-Worth/)** — Tool for tallying assets and liabilities to calculate total net worth.

* **[TD Cash Flow Calculator](https://tools.td.com/Cash-Flow-Calculator/)** — Utility for tracking monthly income and expenses to analyze personal or business cash flow.

* **[TD Return on Capital (ROC) Calculator](https://tools.td.com/ROC-Calculator/):** Calculator for evaluating the efficiency and profitability of capital investments.

* **[TD Debt Management Tool](https://www.tdcanadatrust.com/debtManagement.form?lang=en)** — Resource for structuring, consolidating, and strategizing debt repayment plans.

* **[RBC Monthly Cash Flow Calculator](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/loans-line-of-credit/cash-flow-calculator.html)** — Comprehensive tool for breaking down fixed, variable, and discretionary costs to determine monthly surplus or shortfalls.

* **[RBC TFSA & RRSP Growth Calculators](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/investments/investment-calculators-tools.html)** — Investment and retirement calculators projecting compound growth within registered accounts compared to standard taxable accounts.

* **[Desjardins Personal Balance Sheet Tool](https://www.desjardins.com/en/accounts-services/accesd/financial-insights.html)** — Balance sheet template and tool mapping out current, medium-term, and long-term assets against liabilities to establish precise net worth.

* **[Desjardins Monthly Budget & Activity Tracker](https://www.desjardins.com/en/accounts-services/accesd/financial-insights.html)** — Digital cash flow tracker sorting incoming revenue and spending categories (using models like the 50/30/20 budget rule).

* **[CIBC Budget and Cash Flow Calculator](https://www.cibc.com/en/personal-banking/smart-advice/tools-calculators/budget-cash-flow-calculator.html)** — Interactive calculator modeling after-tax household income against housing, communications, and living expenses to generate tailored portfolio insights.

### 🏠 Canadian Mortgage Calculators & Tools
Specialized calculators for evaluating mortgage payments, home purchase affordability, and amortization schedules from major Canadian financial institutions.

### 🔴 RBC Royal Bank
* **[RBC Mortgage Payment Calculator](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/mortgages/mortgage-calculators.html)** — Interactive tool to calculate monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly mortgage payments based on loan amount, interest rate, term, and payment frequency.
* **[RBC Mortgage Affordability Calculator](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/mortgages/tools/mortgage-affordability-calculator/index.html)** — Evaluates maximum housing budgets, incorporating gross income, existing debts, property taxes, and heating estimates.

### 🔵 Desjardins
* **[Desjardins Mortgage Payment Calculator](https://www.desjardins.com/ca/tools/mortgage-payments/index.jsp)** — Comparative calculator displaying different fixed and variable interest rate scenarios and regular payment amounts.
* **[Desjardins Amortization Calculator](https://www.desjardins.com/ca/tools/amortization-calculator/index.jsp)** — Tool to project payoff timelines, remaining balances, and interest proportions over the life of a loan.

### 🟦 CIBC
* **[CIBC Mortgage Payment & Amortization Calculator](https://www.cibc.com/en/personal-banking/mortgages/calculators.html)** — Instant estimation tool for examining regular mortgage commitments and corresponding amortization schedules.
* **[CIBC Mortgage Affordability Calculator](https://www.cibc.com/en/personal-banking/mortgages/calculators/affordability-calculator.html)** — Comprehensive home price range estimator accounting for minimum down payments, gross household income, and liabilities.
