## 📡 Live Vehicle & Traffic Tracking
Real-time telemetry, radar maps, and tracking platforms for aviation, maritime vessels, rail networks, public transit, and orbital satellites.

### ✈️ Aviation & Flight Tracking

- **[FlightAware](https://www.flightaware.com/)** — Comprehensive global flight tracking platform offering real-time commercial and private aviation data, airport delays, and historical logs.

- **[Flightradar24](https://www.flightradar24.com/)** — Popular live air traffic map combining ADS-B, MLAT, and radar data with detailed aircraft telemetry and 3D flight views.

- **[ADS-B Exchange](https://www.adsbexchange.com/)** — Unfiltered, community-driven flight tracking network displaying military, private, and government aircraft often blocked on commercial trackers.

- **[Flighty](https://www.flighty.com/)** — Passenger-focused flight tracking app providing ultra-fast delay predictions, inbound aircraft monitoring, and live airport alerts.

- **[The Air Traffic](https://theairtraffic.com/)** — Open-source, unfiltered ADS-B feeder and live air traffic map built for aviation enthusiasts and real-time airspace monitoring.

- **[Plane Finder](https://planefinder.net/)** — Real-time global flight tracker featuring clean map visualizations, aircraft playback history, and detailed routing information.

### 🚢 Maritime & Vessel Tracking

- **[MarineTraffic](https://www.marinetraffic.com/)** — Industry-leading AIS ship tracking platform providing live vessel positions, port activity, and maritime fleet analytics.

- **[VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/)** — Real-time global ship tracker utilizing a vast terrestrial and satellite AIS network to monitor cargo ships, tankers, and yachts.

- **[MyShipTracking](https://www.myshiptracking.com/)** — Live maritime traffic map offering real-time AIS vessel positions, port arrival/departure schedules, and fleet tracking tools.

### 🚆 Rail & Public Transit

- **[TRAVIC (Transit Visualization Client)](https://travic.app/)** — Live global public transit visualization map displaying real-time movements of trains, trams, subways, and buses based on GTFS feeds.

- **[OpenRailwayMap](https://www.openrailwaymap.org/)** — Detailed, crowdsourced world map of railway infrastructure, tracks, signaling systems, and speed limits built on OpenStreetMap data.


### 🛰️ Space & Satellites
- **[Heavens-Above](https://www.heavens-above.com/)** — Orbital tracking database providing real-time ground tracks, star charts, and visibility predictions for the ISS, Starlink, and other satellites.
