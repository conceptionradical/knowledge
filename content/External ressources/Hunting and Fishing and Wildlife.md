## 🦌 Access & Landowner Platforms
* **[Mirador](https://www.mirador.ca/)** — Official platform (by the Fédération québécoise des chasseurs et pêcheurs) designed to connect hunters and outdoor enthusiasts with private landowners for secure, authorized access to land.

* **[Le Mythe des Grands Espaces](https://lemythedesgrandsespaces.jimdofree.com/accueil/)** — Critical essay and commentary platform examining the socioeconomic realities, land accessibility barriers, commercialization, and privatization challenges associated with outdoor recreation and public lands in Québec. *(Note: Critical reference site for territorial access analysis)*

## 📜 Regulations, Education & Associations

* **[Fédération québécoise des chasseurs et pêcheurs (Fédécp) - Éducation](https://fedecp.com/la-chasse/je-pratique/contenu-educatif/)** — Educational content, safety guidelines, and best practices for hunting in Québec.

* **[Ministère de l'Environnement, de la Lutte contre les changements climatiques, de la Faune et des Parcs (MELCCFP)](https://mffp.gouv.qc.ca/)** — Official provincial authority portal for wildlife management, hunting regulations, and environmental compliance. *(Note: Formerly MFFP)*

## 🌲 Territory Sharing & Forest Mapping

* **[Partageons la forêt - Outils de communication](http://www.partageonslaforet.com/outils_communiquer.html)** — Resources and communication tools for safe cohabitation and coordination between forest users, industry operators, and hunters.

* **[MERN / MRNF - Carte Interactive (Rde)](https://appli.mern.gouv.qc.ca/Rde/Info/CarteInteractive)** — Interactive Québec government mapping tool for exploring public and private land, land rights, and territorial data.

## 🔪 Field Processing, Butchering & Maintenance

* **[FédéCP Wild Game Butchering Resource](https://fedecp.com/)** — Official instructional media and educational content from the Fédération québécoise des chasseurs et pêcheurs covering proper techniques for field dressing, hygiene, quartering, and processing game meat. *(Note: Supplementary video resource covering field-to-table handling)*

* **[Lansky Guide: Which Angle Should I Use When Sharpening a Knife?](https://www.lansky.com/resources/post/which-angle-should-i-use-when-sharpening-knife.html)** — Official breakdown of preset sharpening angles (17°, 20°, 25°, and 30°) and how to match them to specific blade types, from delicate skinners to heavy-duty camp knives.
