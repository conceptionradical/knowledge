## ⚙️ Industrial Components & Hardware
Essential suppliers and libraries providing native CAD models for off-the-shelf mechanical parts, fasteners, and hardware.

- **[McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/)** — The gold standard for readily available industrial hardware, raw materials, and mechanical components with direct 3D CAD downloads.

- **[Essentra Components](https://www.essentracomponents.com/)** — Manufacturer of essential industrial components, protection caps, fasteners, and hardware.

- **[Lincoln Electric CAD Library](https://www.lincolnelectric.com/)** — Standard components and weldment models for structural and joining applications.

- **[Quebox / Quebox Industrial Components](http://www.quebox.eu/)** — Specialized industrial and manufacturing components. *(Broken / Offline Link - For Future Search)*

## 📐 General CAD & Engineering Libraries
Broad multi-format repositories for mechanical parts, supplier catalogs, and community-shared models.
- **[SketchUp 3D Warehouse](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/)** — Massive open library of user-created and manufacturer 3D models for architecture, design, and visualization.

- **[GrabCAD Community Library](https://grabcad.com/library)** — A massive community-driven library of user-submitted 3D models, assemblies, and engineering challenges.

- **[TraceParts Online](https://www.traceparts.com/)** — Comprehensive digital engineering library featuring millions of supplier-certified CAD models.

- **[3D ContentCentral](https://www.3dcontentcentral.com/)** — Free library of user- and supplier-certified 3D parts, assemblies, and 2D blocks (heavy SolidWorks integration).

- **[PARTcommunity](https://www.partcommunity.com/)** — 3D CAD models portal featuring certified supplier catalogs for mechanical, electrical, and pneumatic parts.

- **[Thangs](https://thangs.com/)** — Geometric search engine and repository for 3D models and CAD files.

- **[3DCADBrowser](https://www.3dcadbrowser.com/)** — Large database of 3D models for engineering, game development, and visualization.

- **[CAD Corner](https://cad-corner.com/)** — Engineering resources, free AutoCAD blocks, DWG drawings, and technical drafting details.

- **[Innerscene Drawing Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/drawings)** — Repository of free, real-scale CAD drawings, details, and layout templates released under CC0.
-
- **[Innerscene 3D Parts Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts)** — Open collection of public domain 3D models, mechanical components, and furniture for visualization and prototyping.

## 🏗️ Architecture, BIM & Construction
Resources tailored for building envelopes, structural components, and architectural integration.
- **[Hilti BIM/CAD Library](https://www.hilti.com/)** — Direct CAD and BIM models for anchoring, fastening, and firestopping systems.

- **[BIMobject](https://www.bimobject.com/)** — Global marketplace and repository for manufacturer-specific BIM objects and architectural models.

- **[CADdetails](https://www.caddetails.com/)** — High-quality architectural building product information, CAD drawings, and 3D models.

- **[ARCAT](https://www.arcat.com/)** — Building product information, specifications, and CAD/BIM details for architects and engineers.
