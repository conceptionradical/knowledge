## ⚡ Welding & Fabrication Resources

* **[Lincoln Electric Resource Library](https://www.lincolnelectric.com/en-us/support/Pages/resources.aspx)** — Comprehensive technical support hub featuring welding safety guides, procedure handbooks, calculators, metallurgy data, and expert knowledge for arc welding processes.

* **[Sparta Engineering Weld Specification Guide](https://www.spartaengineering.com/w59-how-to-spec-a-weld/)** — Practical guide on how to specify welds according to structural standards (CSA W59 / AWS).

* **[Weld It Myself Flux-Core vs. MIG Guide](https://welditmyself.com/flux-core-welding-vs-mig/)** — Comparative breakdown of flux-core arc welding (FCAW) and gas metal arc welding (GMAW/MIG).
