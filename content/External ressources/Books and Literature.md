## 💻 E-Book Management & Software

* **[Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/)** — The essential, open-source one-stop solution for e-book management, format conversion, metadata editing, and device syncing.

* **[Thorium Browser](https://thorium.rocks/)** — High-performance, open-source Chromium fork built with aggressive compiler optimizations (AVX, SSE4) for maximum browsing speed, multi-platform support, and enhanced privacy.

## 📚 Open Digital Libraries & Archives
* **[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/)** — Open, editable library catalog aiming to create a web page for every book ever published.

* **[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/)** — Massive database of over 60,000 free public domain e-books.

* **[Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/)** — Digital library of the National Library of France (*Bibliothèque nationale de France*) and its partners.

* **[Canadiana](https://www.canadiana.ca/)** — Heritage content, historical documents, and books documenting Canadian history.

## 🏛️ Regional & Municipal Collections (Québec)

* **[Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec (BAnQ)](https://www.banq.qc.ca/)** — Provincial library and national archives of Québec.

* **[Bibliothèque de Québec - Collections Numériques](https://www.bibliothequedequebec.qc.ca/collectionsnumeriques/livres.aspx)** — Access portal for digital books, audiobooks, and media databases.

* **[Archives de la Ville de Québec (Moteur de recherche)](https://www.ville.quebec.qc.ca/citoyens/archives/)** — Full-text historical search engine for the fonds and collections of the City of Québec archives.

* **[Prêt Numérique](https://www.pretnumerique.ca/)** — Free digital and audio book lending platform for participating libraries.

## 🧒 Specialized & Accessibility Reading

* **[BibliOdyssée](https://www.bibliodyssee.ca/)** — Digital collection of books specially adapted for children and adolescents (ages 6–15) with reading difficulties or dyslexia.
