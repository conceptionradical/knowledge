## 🇨🇦 Canadian Federal Legislation

* **[Justice Laws Website](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/)** — The official online source for consolidated federal Acts and regulations of Canada, maintained by the Department of Justice.

* **[Canada.ca Treaties, Laws and Regulations](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/laws.html)** — Portal for government acts, regulations, constitutional documents, and international agreements.

* **[Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) — Environmental Law and Legislation](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/corporate/international-affairs/environmental-law-legislation.html)** — The main federal portal outlining federal environmental statutes, including the *Species at Risk Act (SARA)* and the *Migratory Birds Convention Act*.

## ⚜️ Québec Legislation & Case Law

* **[SOQUIJ (Société québécoise d'information juridique)](https://citoyens.soquij.qc.ca)** — Free public portal for searching Québec jurisprudence, court decisions, and legal documentation.

* **[LegisQuébec](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/)** — The official and up-to-date repository for consolidated statutes and regulations of Québec.

* **[Endroit.ca](https://www.endroit.ca/)** — Formerly `justice-quebec.ca`, providing access to Québec justice system resources and legal information.

* **[Portail des Entreprises Québec - Permis et Licences](https://www2.gouv.qc.ca/entreprises/portail/quebec/recherche?lang=fr&x=permis)** — Official provincial search tool for business permits, licenses, and regulatory compliance in Québec.

* **[Publications Québec — Recueil des lois et des règlements du Québec](https://www.publicationsquebec.gouv.qc.ca/)** — The official legal publisher for all consolidated provincial laws and regulations.

* **[MELCCFP — Réglementation Faune et Chasse](https://www.environnement.gouv.qc.ca/)** — Official regulatory authority pages and tools governing wildlife conservation, hunting/fishing zones, and environmental compliance.

* **[Registre foncier du Québec en ligne](https://www.registrefoncier.gouv.qc.ca/)** — The official Québec government registry for land titles, property rights, official cadastre, and territorial descriptions.
