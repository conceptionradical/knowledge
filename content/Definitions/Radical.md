### **Etymology**: From Latin *radic-*, *radix* ("root"). Core conceptual meaning: *Going to the root; fundamental; originating from the base.*

---

## Phonetic Transcriptions & Definitions by Domain

### 1. Mathematics
* **Pronunciation**: ` /ˈradək(ə)l/ ` *(Standard modern IPA with a schwa)*
* **Part of Speech**: Adjective
* **Definition**: Of or relating to the root of a number or quantity.

### 2. Chemistry (Compounds)
* **Pronunciation**: ` /răd′ĭ-kəl/ ` *(American Heritage standard key with short-vowel marks)*
* **Part of Speech**: Noun
* **Definition**: A group of atoms behaving as a single unit across a number of compounds.

### 3. Chemistry (Reactive Species)
* **Pronunciation**: ` /ˈræd.ɪ.kəl/ ` or ` /ˈrad.ɪ.kəl/ ` *(Standard IPA with syllable dot separators)*
* **Part of Speech**: Noun
* **Definition**: A molecule or atom possessing an unpaired (extra) electron, making it highly reactive with other molecules (free radical).

### 4. Political, Social & Philosophical Systems
* **Pronunciation**: ` /ˈrædɪkəl/ ` *(International Phonetic Alphabet without secondary marks)*
* **Part of Speech**: Adjective
* **Definition**: Relating to, supporting, or advocating fundamental, structural, or revolutionary changes in current practices, conditions, institutions, or power structures (going to the root of a systemic issue).

### 5. Botany
* **Pronunciation**: ` /'rad-i-kəl/ ` *(Merriam-Webster standard respelling system)*
* **Part of Speech**: Adjective
* **Definition**: Of, or springing direct from, the root or stem base of a plant (e.g., radical leaves).

### 6. Linguistics & Philology
* **Pronunciation**: ` /ˈrædɪkl/ ` *(Narrow IPA transcription featuring a syllabic /l/ ending)*
* **Part of Speech**: Adjective / Denoting
* **Definition**: Denoting or relating to the root forms of words, such as radical morphemes or etymological root stems.

### 7. Cultural & Informal Usage
* **Pronunciation**: ` /ˈræd ɪ kəl/ ` *(Standard American IPA with syllable spaces)*
* **Part of Speech**: Adjective (Informal — North American)
* **Definition**: Very good; excellent; extreme or striking in execution.
